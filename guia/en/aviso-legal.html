What Lurra is

Lurra is a website that builds, in the visitor's browser, a report on a rural parcel from public data services. The report is informative and non-binding: it is not official mapping, a certification, a valuation or a planning report, and it does not replace the nota simple from the Registro de la Propiedad (the Land Registry), information from the town hall or the work of a qualified professional.

Where the data come from

Lurra has no database of its own. Each report is put together on the spot from the responses of these services:

  • Parcels and cadastral data: Dirección General del Catastro (the Spanish cadastre). The report is a derived work and does not replace the cadastral certificate.
  • Land-use units and land use: SIGPAC, Fondo Español de Garantía Agraria (FEGA).
  • Flood zones: Sistema Nacional de Cartografía de Zonas Inundables, Ministerio para la Transición Ecológica y el Reto Demográfico (MITECO), via IDEE. Outside Spain, the GloFAS global river flood hazard map (Copernicus Emergency Management Service and the European Commission's Joint Research Centre).
  • Indicative planning classification: Sistema de Información Urbana (SIU), Ministerio de Vivienda y Agenda Urbana.
  • Natura 2000 and protected natural areas: Inventario Español del Patrimonio Natural y de la Biodiversidad (MITECO). Outside Spain, the European Environment Agency and Protected Planet, the world database of protected areas from UNEP-WCMC and IUCN (www.protectedplanet.net).
  • Terrain and aerial photography: © Instituto Geográfico Nacional de España (MDT and PNOA), CC-BY 4.0, scne.es. Outside Spain, Terrain Tiles (Mapzen and AWS).
  • Vegetation by satellite: contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data, served by Earth Search (Element 84) on AWS.
  • Land cover: Sentinel-2 10 m Land Use/Land Cover, from Impact Observatory, Microsoft and Esri, CC-BY 4.0.
  • Soil type: SoilGrids 2.0, from ISRIC – World Soil Information, CC-BY 4.0.
  • Groundwater: status of groundwater bodies in the river basin management plans, European Environment Agency (WISE).
  • Access and nearby services: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), queried through the public Overpass servers.
  • Livestock trails and public forests: Red General de Vías Pecuarias and Inventario de la propiedad de los montes, from the Inventario Español del Patrimonio Natural y de la Biodiversidad (MITECO). In the United States, public lands: Protected Areas Database of the United States (PAD-US), from the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS).
  • Wildfires: European Forest Fire Information System (EFFIS) and Global Wildfire Information System (GWIS), from Copernicus and the European Commission's Joint Research Centre, CC-BY 4.0.
  • Land price: Encuesta de precios de la tierra (land price survey) of the Ministerio de Agricultura, Pesca y Alimentación (MAPA). In the rest of the European Union, Eurostat (agricultural land prices by region) and GISCO for the statistical region; in the United States, the National Agricultural Statistics Service (USDA NASS); in Canada, Statistics Canada.
  • Base maps: © OpenStreetMap contributors and Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 by EOX IT Services GmbH (contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016), CC-BY 4.0. The place search and the place name in reports outside Spain use Nominatim, from OpenStreetMap.

These bodies do not endorse or review the reports. If a source's data are wrong or out of date, the report will be too.
